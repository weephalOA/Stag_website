Get involved

Joining STAG means doing something practical for people going through hard times, and raising money for charities that need it, alongside other Stirling students. You don't need any experience, and you can give as much or as little time as you have.

What volunteering looks like

  • Care package prep

    Our homeless drives are funded by student memberships. We pack the care packages together, ready for the next run.

  • Distribution runs

    Small groups travel to Glasgow and Edinburgh to hand packages out in person and stop for a chat.

  • Fundraising

    From charity hikes to karaoke nights, we raise money for different charities, like Movember and Stirling & District Women's Aid.

  • Socials

    Not everything is work. Socials are where volunteers become friends.
